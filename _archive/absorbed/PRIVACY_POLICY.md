# Privacy Policy — LPS Home Projects

**Effective Date:** 2026-03-14
**Developer/Owner:** LPS

---

## What the App Does

LPS Home Projects is a personal utility application that connects to the Pinterest API to read the owner's own Pinterest boards and pins. Its sole purpose is to support personal home project documentation — collecting, organizing, and referencing pin data for private use. The app is not a commercial product and is not available to the general public.

---

## Data Collected

The app reads the following data from the authenticated Pinterest account:

- Board names, descriptions, and URLs
- Pin titles, descriptions, image URLs, and source links
- Board and pin identifiers (IDs assigned by Pinterest)

The app does **not** collect:

- Data from any user other than the authenticated account owner
- Passwords or authentication credentials (OAuth tokens are managed by Pinterest's auth flow)
- Location data, device identifiers, or behavioral analytics
- Any data from users who have not authenticated the app

---

## How Data Is Used

All data retrieved from Pinterest is used exclusively for personal home project documentation. Specifically:

- To organize and reference home project inspiration and materials
- To create a local record of saved pins for offline review
- To support personal planning workflows (e.g., materials, suppliers, design references)

No automated decision-making, profiling, or commercial use is performed on any data.

---

## Data Storage

Pin metadata (titles, descriptions, URLs, IDs) is stored locally on the developer's personal machine. No data is transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party services operated by this app. Storage is limited to what is needed for personal reference and is retained only as long as it remains useful.

---

## Third-Party Sharing

LPS Home Projects does **not** share, sell, rent, or transmit any data to third parties. The only third-party service involved is Pinterest itself, which provides the data via its official API under Pinterest's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. This app does not introduce any additional data processors or recipients.
